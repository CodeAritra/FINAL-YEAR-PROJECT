Why this is useful for a RAG safety pipeline specifically 
⚫Contradiction pairs (§2 vs the CS deck, §3 compression vs §10 complaint) 
force the retriever to pull two distant chunks and the reader to arbitrate — 
good for testing hallucination and citation grounding. 
⚫Buried dependencies (§4 timing risk depends on §5 close date; §8 
commitments conflict with the roadmap) test multi-hop retrieval. 
⚫Sensitive-category spread: financials, salaries, layoffs with names, 
unreleased roadmap, security weaknesses, legal exposure, and credential-
shaped strings — so you can tag which chunks should be gated. 
⚫Versioning and supersession (rev 4 supersedes rev 3) let you test 
whether stale chunks get filtered. 
⚫Tables and appendices give you chunk-boundary stress cases where a 
naive splitter severs a header from its data.
